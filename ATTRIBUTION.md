# Attribution

Castr holds documents that other people wrote. This file names them and links to where
they publish. The licence each is reproduced under, and the record of which files are
unchanged and which Castr has modified, are in
[docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## The OpenAPI Initiative

Most files under `lib/examples/openapi/` are the OpenAPI Initiative's, from its learning
site, [OAI/learn.openapis.org](https://github.com/OAI/learn.openapis.org), reproduced under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. The notices file lists the
three that are not.

## The Swagger Petstore authors

`lib/examples/swagger/petstore.yaml` is derived from the Swagger Petstore OpenAPI 3.0
definition published by `swagger-api` on GitHub, at
[swagger-api/swagger-petstore](https://github.com/swagger-api/swagger-petstore), reproduced
under the Apache License 2.0.

## Oak National Academy

The Oak Curriculum API documents under `lib/tests-transforms/__fixtures__/corpus/`, and an
earlier one at `lib/tests-transforms/__fixtures__/arbitrary/oak-api.json`, are Oak National
Academy's, served at <https://open-api.thenational.academy>. They are reproduced under the
Open Government Licence v3.0.

## The publishers of the formats' definitions

The files under `lib/tests-picture/__fixtures__/definitions/` are the published
definitions of the formats Castr reads. Those named `oas-` are the OpenAPI Initiative's,
served at <https://spec.openapis.org/oas/> and reproduced under the Apache License 2.0.
Those named `jsonschema-2020-12-` are the JSON Schema Specification Authors', served at
<https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/meta/> and reproduced under the 3-clause BSD
licence. `jsonschema-draft-07-schema.json` is theirs too, served at
<https://json-schema.org/draft-07/schema> and reproduced under the same licence.
