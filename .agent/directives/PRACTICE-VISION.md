# Vision: the Practice

**Established: 2026-09-06**, expressing the owner-ratified name, two-product frame
and vision topology. The [umbrella vision](VISION.md) relates the Practice to Castr.

## Beneficiaries and impact

The Practice serves engineering organisations, contributors and the agents that
work with them. It provides trustworthy shared intent, discoverable decisions,
clear ownership and verifiable delivery across sessions and tools.

Its outcome is excellent agent and developer experience: the next contributor
can find what governs their work, act within the right boundary, and establish
what reached its beneficiary without reconstructing a conversation archive.

## Product boundary

The Practice includes its portable doctrine, host-specific operating conventions
and supporting agent tools. It remains in this repository under the current
direction. Extraction into another repository is justified only by a real
consumer and ownership boundary, not by a desire to split folders.

Portable Core describes concepts that another Practice-bearing repository can
adopt. Host doctrine describes this repository. Plans and continuity carry
current execution and evidence. Platform adapters carry platform-specific
mechanisms. These layers must remain distinguishable.

Autonomous development is one possible use of the Practice. The product is not
specific to Claude or any other model, scheduler or interface. An adopted
operating design is not evidence that a particular deployment is running.
Operational state belongs to its owning current record.

## Verified claims and fitness

The Practice applies
[verified-claims engineering](../practice-core/decision-records/PDR-135-verified-claims-engineering.md):
each claim names its outcome, boundary and evidence. A configured hook must be
shown to fire; a clean check must be understood at its actual scope; closing a
session must not be mistaken for delivering a programme.

The headline frame is **Practice fitness**, expressed through named observations
rather than an invented composite score:

| Measure                     | Meaning and computing owner                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Gate latency                | Elapsed time of the declared verification chain, including the environment and cache conditions; repository check profiling owns collection.                                                                       |
| Coordination-collision rate | Confirmed collisions divided by observed coordination opportunities in a named window; the collaboration/incident evidence pipeline owns the future computation and must define the observation denominator first. |
| Drift-detection coverage    | Declared invariant classes with an exercised detection mechanism divided by the complete invariant inventory; Practice validators and their behavioural proofs own the computation.                                |

These are measurement definitions, not present measurements. Missing
denominators, missing observation paths and unexercised detectors remain named
gaps; they are not treated as zero or as proof of health. The current roadmap and
implementation carriers are discoverable through the
[Practice bridge](../practice-index.md).

## Development principles

- Organise knowledge by its owner, consumer and lifetime.
- Retain one authoritative home for each current fact and link to it.
- Preserve useful history without making historical instructions a current route.
- Prefer the smallest mechanism that achieves the actual outcome with full quality.
- Use specialist review and executable evidence at the boundary they can prove.
- Keep work, verification and delivery distinct, and make unfinished obligations visible.

[Principles](principles.md), [testing strategy](testing-strategy.md) and
[definition of done](DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md) remain the engineering contract.
