## Concept exploration: what "held" means

**The frame.** The plan's first proof says every kind of construct has an element that
holds it or a cited refusal. "Holds" has been an assertion by the author of both sides.
The harm is to the owner, who approves the amendment on its strength, and to whoever
implements it and finds a construct the structure cannot carry, which spends the last
reworking.

**What changed.** The seat had taken "a prototype of types" to mean that readers belong
to later steps. The reason skill's first gate says the opposite of what the seat did:
it built an apparatus of assurance (an authored map) and avoided the direct trial (a
reader over real documents).

**"Held", as an observation.** A kind is held when a document that uses it is read into
a value, and something an oracle can judge is preserved: for a schema, the value
accepts what a validator of the source accepts; for a boundary, the document written
back is the document read, but for what the text supplies.

**Proposals, each with its warrant and what would show it wrong.**

1. **Write a small JSON Schema reader in the prototype, for both drafts.** Hold its
   output to the 46 hand-written values and to the note's 123 statements, then run it
   over every schema of the Oak document pinned in the corpus. Its list of what it
   cannot read is the computed form of the list of kinds owed. _Warrant:_ the table above. _Shown wrong
   if:_ the reader over the cases and the corpus surfaces no fault in the types that the
   map missed; then the map was enough and an OpenAPI reader is not worth building.
   _Bound:_ one file, one working stretch, ending when the list is printed.
2. **Run the function that finds supplied closures over the same corpus.** It answers
   how often each target can write the closure in the documents Castr exists to serve.
   Nothing answers that today.
3. **Read section 7, section 11 and SPEC-FV-1 of the specification for refusals that
   already cover kinds on the owed list.** Cheap, and it shrinks the list by what is
   already decided.
4. **Only then a reader round,** given the map, the values and the corpus results, not
   the trial's prose.

**Evidence that could change this.** Whether the owner wants the structure approved in
one amendment or in stages; the plan says one, and the seat has not asked.

## Reason: the order, and why

- **The kind of thing.** A decision about the order of evidence, in a complicated
  technical system with one human gate. The question is at the altitude of strategy.
- **The warrant.** External confrontation finds the defects: six kinds of instance in
  this step, against none found by the authored map.
- **Reversibility.** A reader on a branch nothing reads is a door that opens both ways:
  run it. The amendment is close to a one-way door, because an overturn spends the last
  reworking: nothing is drafted until the reader and the reader round are done.
- **The strongest case against.** The plan's proofs are the contract, and a reader is
  a later step's work, so building one delays the amendment. The answer: authoring
  elements for 81 kinds and 57 formats blind costs more than a reader that says which
  of them real documents use, and the plan itself says a case the structure cannot hold
  changes the structure.
- **A fault the pass found in the seat's own code.** `closures.ts` says Zod cannot
  write any closure that a choice reaches. As a general statement that is false. This
  is a prediction and has not been run: an `anyOf` by value is the union of its strict
  branches and of the intersection of each set of them; a `oneOf` is writable as an
  exclusive union of strict branches wherever no value that two branches accept, as
  their source states them, fits within the fields of one. The function's own test
  asserts the false verdict. It is the first thing to run against Zod.

## Parallax, at core depth

Run in this one context (`emulated-reduced`), so its challenge is a self-review and
satisfies no requirement for independent assurance.

- **The question.** Is the third trial ready for a reader round and then the amendment,
  and what evidence comes next?
- **The frame, and its serious rival.** The seat's: shapes as an algebra with
  accounting, the profile by value, boundaries as parts and edges. The rival: each
  format's constructs under neutral names, with meaning left to the writers. The plan
  says the rival must fail one of proofs 2, 3 and 6. **Much of the boundary half is the
  rival.** The check finds 125 elements only OpenAPI states, and the seat passed them
  with one sentence. Where a description states plain data (a contact, a licence, a
  title) the element is that datum and should say so; where it states meaning (what
  carries a value, an encoding's mode, security as a choice of requirements, a status)
  the element was designed. The list needs its groups and a sentence for each.
- **Scales.** One schema and one value; composition across definitions and resources;
  the amendment; the validators Castr generates for the Ecosystem's documents; the
  corpus proofs of SPEC-G-4.
- **Bridges with nothing under them.** From "holds the 70 cases" to "holds real
  documents": the cases are the seat's own. From "by value is the meaning" to "the
  Ecosystem's documents can be written in Zod": not measured. From "the types exist" to
  "a reader and a writer can be built on them": shown in part for shapes, not at all
  for boundaries.
- **Evidence that is not independent.** The readers, the advisor and the hosted
  reviewers all read the seat's own texts and briefs. The runs against Hyperjump and
  Zod are the only evidence that owes nothing to the seat's account.
- **Status: provisional.** It permits the evidence work above. It forbids drafting the
  amendment as if a proof were met, and calling any proof met.
- **Return to the world.** The reader and the corpus run of proposal 1. Expected: its
  values equal the hand-written ones; what it cannot read is within the list owed.
  Reopen the structure if the prototype disagrees with the validator on any corpus
  schema, or if a corpus construct needs a new kind of node.
