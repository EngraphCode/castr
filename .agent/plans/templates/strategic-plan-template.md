# Strategic-plan template

The why and the what: the outcome, the bet it serves, what success
looks like, and the tempo of the subtree. No implementation detail, no
todos. Copy, fill, delete the guidance. The owner-directive block opens
the frontmatter so the warrant is visible at the top of every
plan, always.

```markdown
---
title: <Human name — the outcome this strategic brief pursues>
status: future
lane: future
created: <YYYY-MM-DD>
last_updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
owner_directive: >-
  <The owner words that commissioned or ratified this direction —
  verbatim where quoted.>
promotion_trigger: >-
  <The named condition that promotes this brief into a current/
  executable plan.>
---

# <Name>

## Outcome

<What is true in the world when this bet pays off — stated as an
observable state, not an activity.>

## The bet

<Why this outcome, why this way, and what we are deliberately not
doing instead.>

## Success looks like

<The evidence that would show the outcome reached — named honestly,
including what this node explicitly does not claim.>

## Delivery

Executable plans serving this brief link back to it from their `owner_directive` or body —
enumerate them by search, never by a hand-kept list. Milestones live
in Linear as named observable states; this node points at them, never
mirrors them.
```
