# Plan templates

One template per plan type, each opening with its ratification block so
sketch-vs-ratified is visible at the top of every plan from birth.
Transplanted 2026-08-24 from the origin estate (OCE) under the owner's
equality directive; in this repository the governing plan architecture
remains ADR-117 (plan templates and components) with the
`active/`/`current/`/`future/` lanes — the origin estate's plan-node
schema and impact-area registry are a later generation, queued as a
future equality instalment rather than half-adopted here.

| Template                                                   | Use for                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`strategic-plan-template.md`](strategic-plan-template.md) | A strategic node: the outcome, the bet, success, and the tempo of its subtree |
| [`delivery-plan-template.md`](delivery-plan-template.md)   | One step of a lane, authored by its implementer at pickup                     |
| [`runbook-plan-template.md`](runbook-plan-template.md)     | A repeatable operational procedure                                            |

Copy the skeleton from inside the template's fenced block, fill it,
delete the guidance. Every plan is born `status: sketch` and governs no
work until its ratification stamp is complete.

The `components/` library (quality gates, lifecycle triggers, TDD
phases, risk assessment, and companions) is live referenced substrate
for plans authored here; the plan skill's component references resolve
into it. Origin-estate history notes (its pre-D23 dispositions and
ADR-216 plan-node doctrine) stay in OCE — they are cited, not copied.
