preservation coverage is the compiler headline metric, with no published

## Impact and authority

Compiler consumers need demonstrably faithful application-value and interaction
contracts. Engineering organisations and agents need a portable, reliable
Practice whose claims can be checked. Strategy and documentation must distinguish
the ratified target, implemented behaviour and proven scope.

Permanent authority lives in the foundational directives, ADRs and portable
PDRs. The plan architecture is governed by PDR-018 and
[the local templates](../templates/README.md). Foreign ADR-117/ADR-216 were cited
lineage, not adopted local authority; no import is required to repair the plan
skill. The original July proposal is
[conserved verbatim](../../memory/operational/archive/plan-estate-2026-09-06.md).

## Residual implementation and structural work

### W3 validator — programme Q-12

Build the doctrine-claims validator with two independent capabilities:

1. Resolve proof anchors supporting claimed capabilities in requirements,
   vision and roadmap.
2. Detect contradictory statuses across the corresponding claim-bearing surfaces.

Use the real contradiction instances as red-first fixtures; prove failures for
missing anchors and conflicting claims, then green on the corrected estate.
The refresh delivery's inherited-reference appendix supplies 77 unresolved link
occurrences across 45 untouched files. Q-12's reference-closure slice must
adjudicate their local authority/provenance, repair destinations and preserve
the existing scoped-blocking TC3b intent without silently masking archive
handling. They are not corrected by the present documentary slice.

Wire the validator into the blocking repository validator chain and prove that
the integration rejects a seeded contradiction. No prose-only completion or
green unrelated suite substitutes for this consumer proof. Q-12 must materialise
the bounded implementation row when its horizon opens.
